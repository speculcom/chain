Payments & settlement

Visa TAP: letting merchants recognise trusted shopping agents

The intersection: TAP answers “how does a merchant know this agent is really consumer-authorised, not a malicious bot?” It adds payment semantics on top of Web Bot Auth HTTP Message Signatures: a signed request can distinguish browse from pay, bind the consumer behind the agent, and declare the expected payment method (network token, guest checkout or micropayment).
Scope: co-created by Visa and Cloudflare, released on 14 October 2025 in the Visa Developer Center and GitHub, with feedback from Adyen, Ant International, Checkout.com, Coinbase, CyberSource, Elavon, Fiserv, Microsoft, Nuvei, Shopify, Stripe and Worldpay. By 2026 it is in live transactions: Visa Intelligent Commerce went live in Europe with 30+ issuers and merchants such as lastminute.com, and BILL with Billtrust ran a live B2B invoice-payment pilot using TAP.
Open: TAP is not an on-chain protocol and does not define refund or dispute liability; merchants must add signature verification and a directory, while agents must register and be recognised by issuers/networks; cross-network interoperability still leans on Web Bot Auth and coverage is expanding.
