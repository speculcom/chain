Five layers under the agent economy:
accounts, identity, payments, card networks, x402

ERC-4337 handles custom accounts without consensus changes; ERC-8004 handles why an unknown agent is trusted; AP2 handles authorisation evidence for agent payments; Visa TAP and Mastercard Agent Pay run real card-network transactions; Algorand x402 adds atomic and sponsored transactions to machine payments. The first three have very different status — a mature standard, a Draft and unconfirmed production — while card networks are live but do not replace AP2’s mandate layer.

Separating live / Draft / unconfirmed-production / card-network-live / technical-integration is the point of this page. Do not write “has a spec” as “widely used”, or “card networks are live” as “AP2 is deployed”.

2026-11-23Protocol / payments

Algorand x402: atomic and sponsored transactions

Algorand has extended its x402 support from the February technical integration to atomic and sponsored transactions: multiple actions settle together or fail together; a facilitator or service can cover network fees for gasless payment flows. x402 uses CAIP-2 network identifiers, with Algorand mainnet USDC among the default assets.

Why it matters

The hard part of agent payments is not “can pay” but making payment, authorisation and usage accounting either all hold or all roll back. Algorand’s native atomic grouping puts them into one settlement unit; sponsored transactions let an agent without ALGO pay first. For pay-per-call APIs, paid MCP tools and M2M settlement, it is a notable step in x402’s multi-chain expansion.

Boundary / what it does not claim

Atomic grouping is an Algorand ledger capability, not a new cross-chain x402 standard; sponsored transactions still leave credit and compliance risk with the facilitator. Official pages disclose no live volume, failure rate or merchant count, and the 2026-11-23 report is secondary. It does not claim x402 is at large-scale production on Algorand, or that atomic transactions eliminate payment disputes.

Sources：Algorand x402 official page · Algorand x402 developers · CoinMarketCap report (2026-11-23)

2026-04-28Protocol / payments

AP2: v0.2 and donation to the FIDO Alliance

Google donated AP2 to FIDO and released v0.2 with Human Not Present autonomous payments; Checkout / Payment mandates, each open or closed, signed with SD-JWT.

Why it matters

It writes the scope of agent payment authorisation into verifiable mandates instead of passing a card number; for agent checkout, subscriptions and disputes, it is a required protocol layer.

Boundary / what it does not claim

No publicly confirmed production transaction as of October 2026; v0.2 is only the second release; AP2 does no checkout, catalogue or settlement and must sit inside UCP or another commerce protocol; FIDO stewardship just began. It does not claim scale, or unconditional agent charging.

Sources：Google blog · AP2 FAQ

2026-01-29Protocol / identity

ERC-8004: Trustless Agents goes mainnet

Three singleton registries: Identity (ERC-721 + agent-card JSON with A2A / MCP endpoints), Reputation and Validation; integrates with x402 payments; still Draft, with v2 in development.

Why it matters

It splits “why trust an unknown agent” into three queryable layers instead of “on-chain means trust”. For agent marketplaces, cross-org delegation and payments, it is the first 2026 protocol to read.

Boundary / what it does not claim

Reputation can be sybil-attacked or farmed; validation is pluggable, not on by default; registration proves an entry exists, not capability or legality; cross-chain registrations are self-declared. It does not claim every agent must register, or that registration equals trust.

Sources：8004 official blog · 8004 learn

2025-10Protocol / card networks

Visa TAP + Mastercard Agent Pay: card networks put agent payments into live merchants

Visa released TAP on 14 October 2025: Web Bot Auth HTTP signatures let merchants distinguish browsing from paying, with live European purchases across 30+ issuers and merchants such as lastminute.com in July 2026, and a BILL + Billtrust B2B invoice-payment pilot in October 2026. Mastercard Agent Pay combines KYA registration, a unique Agentic Token per agent, Payment Passkeys and Verifiable Intent; it launched in the US in 2025 and went live across Singapore, Asia-Pacific and Latin America in 2026.

Why it matters

It fills a missing layer: agent payments are not only on-chain. Card networks already run real transactions with signatures, tokens and biometrics. Compared with AP2’s mandate layer, it shows who authorises, who settles and who only has a spec.

Boundary / what it does not claim

TAP and Agent Pay are different standards and cannot substitute for each other; they depend on card networks and issuers, and refunds, disputes and chargebacks stay under existing card rules. AP2 still has no publicly confirmed production transaction as of October 2026; writing “card networks are live” as “AP2 is everywhere” conflates layers.

Sources：Visa TAP launch · Visa Europe live · BILL + Billtrust pilot · Mastercard Agent Pay · Mastercard Trust Framework

2021-09Protocol / account abstraction

ERC-4337: account abstraction without consensus changes

UserOperation + a separate mempool + Bundler + EntryPoint + Paymaster; custom signatures, batching and sponsored gas without touching the consensus layer.

2021-09-29

Why it matters

It is the base that “agent wallets” and sponsorship cannot avoid; understanding the alt mempool and Paymaster is how you tell real on-chain execution from a custodial account.

Boundary / what it does not claim

The alt mempool brings bundler centralisation and censorship risk; Paymaster sponsorship can be abused; EntryPoint versions need separate compatibility checks; avoiding consensus changes leaves EOA + contract underneath. It does not claim guaranteed gasless transactions or the only AA route.

Sources：EIP-4337 spec · ERC-4337 docs
